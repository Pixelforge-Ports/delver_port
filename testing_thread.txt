Delver - PortMaster testing
Porter: Pixelforge Ports (Ronax)

Game: https://store.steampowered.com/app/249630/Delver/
Package: delver.zip
Install:
Update PortMaster, put delver.zip in its autoinstall folder and open PortMaster.
Stay online for the Java 17 and Westonpack downloads if needed.
Supply delver.jar from your owned Windows build (Delver v1.08; Steam depot 249631, manifest 6680730644186394716).
Destination: <ports directory>/delver/gamedata/delver.jar.

In the Steam console, run: download_depot 249630 249631 6680730644186394716
Copy delver.jar from the completed Windows depot download unchanged.
See the installed delver/README.md for the checksum and download instructions.

Controls:

Controls use Delver's native Xbox 360 gamepad layout. The left stick moves and
the right stick looks; use the in-game gamepad options to view or change bindings.
Menu prompts use Xbox button names. **B** jumps during gameplay and goes back
in menus. Select + Start exits through PortMaster.
Two analog sticks are recommended for movement and looking.

The supplied `delver/options.txt` is copied to `delver/save/options.txt` only on
first launch, preserving existing settings and gamepad bindings.
